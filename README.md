# SENTRI: Sentinel Emergency Network for Treatment & Resistance Intelligence

> **"Turning Invisible Data into Global Health Security"**

SENTRI is an elite, decentralized AI framework designed to bridge the catastrophic "data vacuum" in African Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR) surveillance. By integrating informal health networks—pharmacy kiosks, community workers, and rural healers—into a unified intelligence pipeline, SENTRI provides real-time, localized clinical guidance where connectivity ends.

## The Mission
To empower the "last mile" of healthcare with resource-efficient intelligence, ensuring no resistance pattern remains hidden and no life is lost to a preventable infection.

## Technical Foundation
- **Rust Core:** High-performance, memory-safe data bus for edge deployment.
- **Quantized Multi-Agent AI:** Llama-3-8B (INT4) and Whisper engines running on ARM/Raspberry Pi.
- **Hybrid Ingestion:** USSD, SMS, Voice, and Camera OCR pipelines.
- **Localized RAG:** Knowledge graphs indexing African clinical guidelines and informal market trends.
