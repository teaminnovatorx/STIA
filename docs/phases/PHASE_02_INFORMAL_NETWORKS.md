# PHASE 02: INFORMAL NETWORK MAPPING
## Stakeholder Analysis and Flow Validation of the "Hidden" Health Economy

### 1. Mapping the Informal Stakeholders
The African healthcare landscape is a complex, decentralized mesh. SENTRI identifies four key "Informal Nodes":
- **Patent Medicine Vendors (PMVs):** Small-scale drug shops that are the first line of defense in urban slums and rural villages.
- **Community Health Workers (CHWs):** Mobile, grassroots providers who maintain clinical ledgers but lack digital integration.
- **Traditional Healers:** Often the primary source of care for 40-50% of the population in remote areas.
- **Street Vendors:** High-risk nodes where antibiotics are often sold as loose pills without packaging.

### 2. The Patient Journey (Informal Flow)
1. **Symptom Onset:** Patient bypasses the formal hospital due to distance or "informal fees."
2. **PMV Consultation:** Patient describes symptoms verbally.
3. **Transaction:** PMV sells a "Combo" (often a mix of antibiotics like Amoxicillin and Metronidazole) based on price rather than protocol.
4. **Treatment Failure:** If symptoms persist, the patient tries a different vendor.
5. **Tertiary Arrival:** Patient arrives at a hospital with advanced sepsis and a highly resistant infection.

### 3. Critical Research Question
*How can we capture data at Step 2 and Step 3 without disrupting the vendor's livelihood or causing legal fear?*

**Proposed Solution:** A "Trust-Based Ingestion" model where vendors receive value (real-time guidance/stock alerts) in exchange for anonymized symptom/drug logs.
